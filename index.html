Our Story

Designed for real life, made to be loved.

Oolavia began with a simple belief: a good bag should be beautiful and work as hard as you do. We pair refined, timeless design with practical details, such as roomy interiors, secure closures and comfortable straps.

From statement handbags to understated men's wallets, every Oolavia piece is made with careful materials and an eye for the little details that make everyday carry feel special.
